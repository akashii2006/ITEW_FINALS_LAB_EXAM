About BrewBox Coffee Co.

BrewBox Coffee Co. started in 2020 as a small home roastery in Laguna. We partner directly with farmers in Benguet, Sagada, and Batangas to bring you fresh, fairly traded coffee.

Whether you like a bold espresso or a smooth pour-over, we roast in small batches so every cup tastes its best.
